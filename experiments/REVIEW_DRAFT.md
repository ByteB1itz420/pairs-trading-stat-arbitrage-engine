# Real-data experiment - draft for owner review, not yet README claims

The locally frozen protocol lists XOM, CVX, COP, JPM, BAC and WFC; its public GitHub commit followed the first local run, so the public timestamp is not preregistration proof. Historical daily adjusted Open and Close were fetched from Yahoo Finance through yfinance 1.7.0 with auto_adjust=True on 2026-09-28 22:51 UTC (first run). Data is a current-ticker universe with survivorship bias. Yahoo may revise history. Adjustment incorporates dividends, so adjusted prices are total-return-like accounting units, not executable historical raw dollar prices; fill prices and fractional shares are simulation conventions. No locate, short availability, financing margin, taxes, or market impact are modeled. Free-source reuse terms should be checked before republishing raw quotes; raw rows are not committed.

Main train ends 2020-12-31; 2021-22 validation is diagnostic; 2023-25 is the untouched test. An equal-weight six-stock buy-and-hold is a directional comparator, not a market-neutral or risk-matched benchmark; cash is a zero-return comparator. The public [snapshot directory](real_data_snapshot/) now has the [scenario summary](real_data_snapshot/summary.csv), [data manifest](real_data_snapshot/data_manifest.json), and the base-test [screening](real_data_snapshot/test_cost1x_borrow0pct/pair_screening.csv), [selection](real_data_snapshot/test_cost1x_borrow0pct/selection.json), [fills](real_data_snapshot/test_cost1x_borrow0pct/fills.csv), [trades](real_data_snapshot/test_cost1x_borrow0pct/trades.csv), [equity curve](real_data_snapshot/test_cost1x_borrow0pct/equity_curve.csv), [metrics](real_data_snapshot/test_cost1x_borrow0pct/metrics.json), and [hedge-refit log](real_data_snapshot/test_cost1x_borrow0pct/pair_refits.csv). The severe-cost test also has its [trade ledger](real_data_snapshot/test_cost3x_borrow8pct/trades.csv), [fills](real_data_snapshot/test_cost3x_borrow8pct/fills.csv), [equity curve](real_data_snapshot/test_cost3x_borrow8pct/equity_curve.csv), and [metrics](real_data_snapshot/test_cost3x_borrow8pct/metrics.json). The [walk-forward fold outcomes](real_data_snapshot/walk_forward_folds.json) show the three disjoint train/evaluation boundaries, selection, cash/no-pair decisions and comparators. The [walk-forward screening audit](real_data_snapshot/walk_forward_screening_audit.csv) includes all 15 candidate pairs in each disjoint 2020, 2021, and 2022 fold, including the no-pair/cash folds. The 2021 fold also has its [selection](real_data_snapshot/walk_forward_2/selection.json), [pair screen](real_data_snapshot/walk_forward_2/pair_screening.csv), [trades](real_data_snapshot/walk_forward_2/trades.csv), [fills](real_data_snapshot/walk_forward_2/fills.csv), [equity curve](real_data_snapshot/walk_forward_2/equity_curve.csv), and [metrics](real_data_snapshot/walk_forward_2/metrics.json). Other generated artifacts, including validation detail, the no-pair folds' individual selection/metrics files, and the remaining stress-scenario ledgers, remain local and are not yet publicly auditable. Source rows live only in local ignored `results/real_data/adjusted_prices.csv`, identified by SHA256 in the public manifest. The public script re-downloads unless a local snapshot is supplied. Due to live data revisions, a fresh download may not match the originally observed results exactly. Do not silently optimize parameters against the test.

Preliminary observations for discussion: 2021-22 validation net -9.44%, test 2023-25 net +9.42% (gross +11.95%), max drawdown -9.28%, 18 trades. The directional buy-and-hold comparator returned +65.24% in test. 3x transaction costs plus 8% annual borrow made test net -0.83%. The 2020 and 2022 walk-forward folds had no statistically passing pair and stayed cash; 2021 fold net -3.24%. The test result is not evidence of repeatable alpha: the validation loss, no-trade folds, benchmark underperformance, survivorship and execution/borrow uncertainty all matter.

Method notes: Engle-Granger cointegration screens ordered log-price pairs on training-only formation data; Benjamini-Hochberg controls the false-discovery rate across 15 candidate pairs. Lowest adjusted p-value wins if it passes 0.05. Entry and exit z-score uses the trailing 60 sessions, with next-session adjusted open execution, 2 bps commission + 5 bps adverse slippage per leg, and a no-cost gross counterfactual. Cost scenarios change only costs, not the selection or thresholds.
